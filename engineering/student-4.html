Key vocabulary

Word or phraseMeaningExample
first, then, next, after that, finallywords that show the order of the stepsFirst, switch off the pump. Then close the valves.
once / whenafter this step is completeOnce the bolts are tight, refit the guard.
before / after + -inga short way to join two stepsBefore starting the motor, check the oil level.
the passive: is / are + past participlefor processes, when the action is more important than the personThe concrete is poured and then vibrated.
the passive: was / were + past participlefor reports: what was doneThe beam was tested yesterday. The bolts were tightened.
must / must not (mustn't)it is a rule, it is necessary / it is not allowedYou must wear a hard hat. You must not stand under the load.
shouldit is a good idea, the right thing to doYou should check the belt tension every month.
don't have toit is not necessary (but you can if you want)You don't have to book the crane. It's already booked.
it is essential toit is very important toIt is essential to lock out the machine before maintenance.
PPE (personal protective equipment)a hard hat, safety glasses, gloves, a high-visibility (hi-vis) vest, steel-toe boots, hearing protectionPPE must be worn at all times on site.
lockout (to lock out)switching off a machine's power and putting your own lock on the switch, so nobody can start itEach worker puts his or her own lock on the switch.
inspect / checklook at something carefully to find problemsInspect the welds for cracks.
tighten / loosenmake tighter / make less tightTighten the bolts to 120 N·m.
lubricateadd oil or grease so the parts move easilyLubricate the bearings every 500 hours.
adjustchange something a little to make it correctAdjust the belt tension.
replace / install / removeput a new one in place of an old one / put in and connect / take off or take outRemove the old seal and install a new one.

Project: Level P2 slab repair, parking garage, downtown Toronto
From: Priya Nair, Site Engineer

Work completed today

First, the repair area on Level P2 was closed to traffic, and the damaged concrete was removed with jackhammers. Then the exposed reinforcing bars were cleaned and inspected. The new concrete was poured at 14:00 and covered with wet burlap.

Safety

All workers wore hard hats, safety glasses, hi-vis vests and steel-toe boots. During the concrete removal, one worker took off his hearing protection. The foreman stopped the work and reminded the crew that hearing protection must be worn at all times when jackhammers are in use.

The new concrete must be kept wet for at least seven days, as the specification requires. Cars must not park on the repaired area until the concrete reaches its design strength. Once we receive the test results, I will send them to you.

Exercise 7: Modals and the passive

Watch out

  • must = it is necessary, a rule. must not = it is not allowed. don't have to = it is not necessary, you can choose. They are very different!
  • must + verb, no to: You must wear. (But: you have to wear, it is essential to wear.)
  • Passive: is / are + past participle (processes): The bolts are tightened. was / were + past participle (reports): The bolts were tightened.
  • before / after + -ing: before starting, after checking.
