What this lesson is for

Why this matters: on site and in meetings, you often have to explain what a part is, what it is made of and what it does. Clear, simple phrases help your team understand you and avoid costly mistakes.

You will know

  • names of machine parts: shaft, bearing, bracket, bolt, nut, washer, gear, valve
  • names of structural parts: beam, column, slab, footing, truss, brace
  • phrases: is used to, is designed to, consists of, is made of, is attached to, supports, transmits, prevents

You will understand

  • the difference between is used to + verb and is used for + -ing
  • why we say consists of, never is consisted of

You will be able to

  • say what a machine or structure consists of and what the parts are made of
  • explain how the parts are connected and what each one does
  • describe a machine or structure to a new colleague

Common mistakes to watch for

  • The frame is consisted of four tubes. The frame consists of four tubes.
  • The motor is connected with the gearbox. The motor is connected to the gearbox.
  • A washer is used for spread the load. A washer is used to spread the load. / ... is used for spreading the load.
  • The bracket is made in steel. The bracket is made of steel.
  • The guard prevents to touch the belt. The guard prevents people from touching the belt.
  • The axis turns in two bearings. The shaft turns in two bearings. (an axis is a line on a drawing, not a part)

Warm-up

  • What do you design, build or maintain in your job? Is it more mechanical (machines) or structural (buildings, bridges)?
  • Look around the room. Find one thing that is held together with bolts or screws. What does it do?
  • In a meeting in English, what is harder for you: naming a part, or explaining what it does?

Key vocabulary

Describing a part: what it is and what it does

PhraseUse it forExample
is used to + verb
is used for + -ing / noun		the job or purpose of somethingA washer is used to spread the load. A crane is used for lifting heavy loads.
is designed to + verbwhat the engineer planned it to doThe beam is designed to carry 20 kN.
consists ofthe parts that make the whole (active only: not is consisted of)The gearbox consists of two shafts, four gears and a housing.
is made ofthe materialThe bracket is made of stainless steel.
is attached to / is fixed toone part held on another partThe motor is attached to the frame with four bolts.
is connected toa link between two parts (mechanical, electrical or structural)Each beam is connected to a column.
supportsholds something up or holds it in positionTwo bearings support the shaft.
transmitspasses power, force or movement from one part to anotherThe shaft transmits power from the motor to the pump.
prevents ... from + -ingstops something from happeningThe guard prevents fingers from touching the belt.

Parts of machines

PartWhat it is / what it does
shafta long, round bar that turns and transmits power
bearingsupports a turning shaft and lets it turn with low friction
bracketa small metal part, often L-shaped, fixed to a wall or frame to hold something
bolt / nut / washera bolt goes through the holes; the nut screws onto the bolt; the washer is a flat ring under the nut that spreads the load
welda joint made by melting two metal parts together
geara wheel with teeth; gears transmit turning movement and change speed
springstores energy and absorbs shocks, then returns to its shape
valveopens, closes or controls the flow of a liquid or gas

Parts of structures

PartWhat it is / what it does
beama horizontal member that carries loads across a span to the supports
columna vertical member that carries loads down to the foundation
slaba flat, horizontal layer of concrete that forms a floor or roof
jointthe place where two parts meet or are connected
footingthe wide base under a column or wall that spreads the load into the soil
trussa frame made of triangles, used for roofs and bridges
bracea diagonal member that makes a frame stiffer against wind and other sideways loads

Hi Daniela,

Welcome to the maintenance team! Before your first visit to Line 3 on Monday, here is a short description of the new conveyor.

The conveyor is used to move boxes from the packing area to the loading dock. It consists of a rubber belt, two drums, a frame and an electric motor. The motor is connected to the drive drum through a small gearbox, which transmits power to the drum and reduces the speed. Each drum turns on a shaft, and each shaft is supported by two bearings. Under the belt there are rollers, which support the belt and the boxes.

The frame is made of aluminium, so it is light and easy to move. It is attached to the floor with steel brackets and anchor bolts. At the motor end, a metal guard covers the drum. Exercise 7: Prepositions and mistakes

Watch out

  • of: consists of, is made of, is part of
  • to: is attached to, is connected to, is welded to, is designed to + verb
  • for: is used for + -ing, is suitable for, is responsible for
