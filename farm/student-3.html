What this lesson is for

You will be able to

  • name common farm machines and garden tools
  • explain what a machine or tool does and what it is used for
  • say why people do things: She bought a quad bike to check the fences.

You will know

  • tractor, plough, seed drill, combine harvester
  • spade, pitchfork, hoe, rake, wheelbarrow
  • is used to, is used for, in order to, so that

You will understand

  • is used to + verb, is used for + -ing
  • I came here to learn (not for learn)

Common mistakes to watch for

  • A spade is used for dig. A spade is used for digging. / A spade is used to dig.
  • I came here for learn about farms. I came here to learn about farms.
  • He wears gloves for to protect his hands. He wears gloves to protect his hands.
  • The machine for harvest wheat is a combine. The machine for harvesting wheat is a combine.
  • We cover the hay so that it stay dry. We cover the hay so that it stays dry.
  • Pass me a shear, please. Pass me the shears, please. (always plural, like scissors)

Warm-up

  • Which farm machines can you name in English? Have you ever sat on a tractor?
  • Do you have a garden or a balcony with plants? Which tools do you use?
  • Think of one tool in your kitchen. What is it for? Try to explain it without saying its name.

Key vocabulary

Farm machines

MachineWhat it does
tractora strong vehicle that pulls other machines and trailers
trailera cart pulled behind a tractor; it carries hay, crops or animals
plough (US/Canada plow)turns over the soil before planting
harrowbreaks the soil into small pieces after ploughing
seed drillputs seeds into the ground in neat rows
sprayersprays water, fertiliser or chemicals on crops
combine harvester (a combine)cuts grain crops and separates the grain from the straw
balerpresses hay or straw into bales (big round or square blocks)
quad bike (US/Canada ATV)a small motorbike with four wheels for getting around the farm

Hand tools

ToolWhat it is used for
spadea flat, sharp blade on a handle, for digging
shovela wide, curved blade, for moving soil, sand or snow
pitchforka long fork, for lifting hay and straw
hoea thin, flat blade on a long handle, for removing weeds
rakea row of metal or plastic teeth, for collecting leaves and making soil smooth
wheelbarrowa small cart with one wheel, for carrying soil, stones or manure
shearsvery big scissors, for cutting hedges or sheep's wool (always plural)
bucketa round container with a handle, for carrying water or feed

Saying what it's for

PhraseUse it forExample
is used to + verbthe job of a tool or machineA harrow is used to break up the soil.
is used for + -ing / nounthe same idea, with -ing or a nounA pitchfork is used for lifting hay. This trailer is for animals.
to + verbwhy someone does somethingShe bought a quad bike to check the fences.
in order to + verbthe same, more formalFarmers rotate crops in order to keep the soil healthy.
so that + subject + verbthe result you want; often with can or willWe cover the hay so that it stays dry.

Say it right: plough rhymes with cow; a COMbine (the machine) has stress on the first part, but to comBINE (mix) has stress on the second; hoe sounds like go; SHOvel rhymes with love-l; shears rhymes with ears.

Exercise 7: Purpose and mistakes

Part A: used to ... or used for ...?

Watch out

  • used to + verb: A spade is used to dig holes.
  • used for + -ing: A spade is used for digging holes.
  • Both are correct and mean the same. Never used for dig or used to digging (for a tool's job).

Which phrase goes with each ending? Sort them.
