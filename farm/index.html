Farm English

8 lessons on farm life and farm words for any curious learner: animals, buildings and land, machines, crops and the farming year, looking after animals, food from farm to table, weather and problems, and farm idioms. Each has an interactive student page (games, a reading and an answer key), teacher notes with a 50-minute plan, and 20 practice questions.
