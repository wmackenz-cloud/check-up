English speaking practice · Compare two pictures

Feelings in public places

These pictures show people in different situations. Compare the pictures, and say how the people might be feeling and why. Try to talk for about a minute.

Questions

  1. What is similar about the two pictures? What is different?
  2. In which picture are the people under more pressure? Why do you think so?
  3. Which situation would you prefer to be in? Why?

Useful language for comparing

Both pictures show... · In the first picture..., whereas in the second... · The people in the first picture look more..., while... · One big difference is...

They might be... · She must be... because... · It looks as if...
