A goodbye party at work

Look at the picture. Talk about it for about a minute, then answer the questions. There are no wrong answers: say what you think, and give a reason.

Questions

  1. Describe what you can see in the picture.
  2. Why might Anna be leaving her job? Give two possible reasons.
  3. How do you think Anna feels? Why?
  4. Why might the man by the window not be joining in?
  5. What do you think Anna's colleagues will say to her?
  6. What usually happens when someone leaves a job in your country?
  7. Talk about a time when you said goodbye to someone important.

Useful language

Describing: In this picture I can see... · In the background / foreground... · On the left / right...

Guessing: It looks like... · She might be... · He must be... because... · They can't be... · I'm not sure, but maybe...

Feelings: She looks worried / relieved / emotional · He seems to be in a hurry
