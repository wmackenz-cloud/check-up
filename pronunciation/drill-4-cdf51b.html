Joining Words

Goal: Link words together smoothly. Joining also makes word endings easier, because the last sound moves to the next word.

Time: 5 to 10 minutes a day. Goes with: Lesson 5: Join the Words.

Drill A: Ending to vowel

Drill C: Vowel to vowel

When one word ends in a vowel and the next starts with a vowel, add a tiny w or y between them.
