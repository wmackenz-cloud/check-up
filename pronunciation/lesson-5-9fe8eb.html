Join the Words

Goal: Connect words smoothly, the way English speakers do. Joining words also helps you say the end sounds you practised in Assignment 2.

Time: 10 minutes a day

Loading…

The main rule

When a word ends with a consonant and the next word starts with a vowel, move the consonant to the next word.

worked in sounds like work-tin

pick it up sounds like pi-ki-tup

This makes the end sound easier, because it becomes the start of the next sound.

Part B: Same sound twice

When one word ends with the same sound the next word starts with, say it once but a little longer.

  • bus stop: bu-sstop
  • big game: bi-ggame
  • good day: goo-dday
  • this sentence: thi-ssentence

Part C: Sentences

First read each sentence word by word. Then read it again, joining the words.

  • I worked in an office for a year.
  • Can I have an orange, please?
  • She lived in a small town.
  • Let's meet at eight o'clock.
  • First of all, I'd like to talk about it.
