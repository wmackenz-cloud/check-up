My recordings · Pronunciation drill 3

s, sh and z

Goal: Keep s, sh and z apart, so see does not sound like she and price does not sound like prize.

Time: 5 to 10 minutes a day. Goes with: Lesson 3: Which One Did I Say?

Loading…

Drill A: Lips do the work

s: smile, lips flat, like a snake: sssss.

sh: push your lips forward and round, like telling someone to be quiet: shhhhh.

z: the same as s, but your throat buzzes, like a bee: zzzzz. Put your hand on your throat to feel it.

Switch slowly: sss, shhh, sss, shhh. Then sss, zzz, sss, zzz. Watch your lips in a mirror.
