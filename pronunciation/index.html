    How to record

    1. Press Record. The first time, your browser asks to use the microphone. Choose Allow.
    2. Speak, then press Stop.
    3. Listen. If you are happy, press Save and send to Bill. If not, press Record again.

    Works on phones, tablets and computers in Chrome, Safari or Edge. Use headphones with a microphone in a quiet room for the clearest sound.

    Recordings are saved to Bill's private Google Drive.
