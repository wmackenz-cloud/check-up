Finish the Word

Goal: Say the last sound of every word clearly. In English, the end of a word often tells the listener the time (past or present) and the number (one or more).

Time: 10 minutes a day

Why endings matter

Listen to the difference:

  • I work in a shop. (now)
  • I worked in a shop. (in the past)

If the listener cannot hear -ed, they do not know when it happened.

Part A: Past tense endings

The -ed ending has three sounds. Read each row across, from left to right. Make the last sound strong.

Tip: -ed only makes an extra syllable after a t or d sound. Worked is one syllable, not "work-ed".
