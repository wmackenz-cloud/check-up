Shadow and Speak

Goal: Practise everything together: endings, sounds, rhythm and joining words. First you copy a real English speaker. Then you answer an exam-style question and check your own speaking.

Time: 5 minutes of shadowing every day, plus one speaking question each week

Part A: Shadowing (every day)

Shadowing means listening to a speaker and repeating each sentence, copying the speed, rhythm and sounds.

  1. Choose a short episode of 6 Minute English from BBC Learning English. Each episode has a transcript.
  2. Play one sentence, then pause.
  3. Say the same sentence. Copy the speaker exactly, like an echo.
  4. Do this for 5 minutes. You do not need to finish the episode.
  5. When you are comfortable, try speaking at the same time as the speaker without pausing.

Tip: If one word is difficult, type it into YouGlish to hear it in many real videos.

Part C: Exam speaking (once a week)

Choose one question. Record a 30-second answer. Speak naturally; do not read from notes.

  • Describe a place you visited last year.
  • Tell me about a person who helped you.
  • What did you do on your last holiday?
  • Describe your favourite room in your home.
