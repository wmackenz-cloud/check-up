The th Sound

Goal: Hear the th sound and say it clearly, so think does not sound like tink or sink.

Time: about 15 minutes. Do it once soon after our lesson, and do Parts 4 and 5 again the day before the next one. Goes with: Lesson 3: Which One Did I Say? and Drill 2: The th Sound.

Part 1: Listen and choose

Part 2: Quiet th or buzzing th?

Say each word with your hand on your throat. Quiet th: no buzz, just air. Buzzing th: your throat buzzes. Small words like the, this, that, than are buzzing.

Tap a word, then tap the column it belongs in. Right answers stay; wrong ones jump back.
