Tap the Rhythm

Goal: Speak with English rhythm. In English, important words are strong and long. Small words are weak and short. This makes your English sound natural and easy to follow.

Time: 10 minutes a day

Strong words and weak words

Strong words carry the meaning: nouns, main verbs, adjectives, numbers, question words, and not.

Weak words hold the sentence together: a, the, to, of, and, in, at, for, is, are, was, can.

Example. The strong words are in capitals:

I WORKED in a SMALL SHOP.

If you say only the strong words, the meaning is still clear: worked, small shop.

Part A: Tap and hum

For each sentence:

  1. Tap the table on each strong word.
  2. Hum the rhythm without words: da-DUM-da-da-DUM-DUM.
  3. Say the sentence with the same rhythm.
  • I WORKED in a SMALL SHOP.
  • I WANT to STUDY BUSINESS.
  • We WENT to the BEACH on SUNDAY.
  • WHERE do you LIVE?
  • I'd LIKE a CUP of COFFEE.
  • It was a VERY GOOD DAY.
