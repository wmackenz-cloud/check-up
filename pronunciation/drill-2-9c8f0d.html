The th Sound

Goal: Make a clear th, so think does not sound like tink and they does not sound like day.

Time: 5 to 10 minutes a day. Goes with: Lesson 3: Which One Did I Say?

Loading…

Drill A: Find the position

Tongue out, then air.

1. Put the tip of your tongue lightly between your teeth. Check in a mirror or your camera: you should see it.

2. Blow air out gently: thhhh. This is the th in think and three.

3. Now add your voice, so your throat buzzes: thhhh. This is the th in the, they and mother.

Hold each sound for three seconds: quiet th, then buzzing th. Do it five times.

Drill D: Numbers and dates

These come up in every exam and every conversation.

  • 3, 13, 30, 33, 300
  • the 3rd (third), the 13th (thirteenth), the 30th (thirtieth)
  • the third of March, the thirteenth of May, the thirtieth of June
  • My birthday is on the ... (say yours)

Drill E: Challenge sentences

Slowly first. Then at normal speed.

  • I think there are thirty-three things in the bathroom.
  • My mother and father thought it was a thin book.
  • Thank you for the third time this month.
  • They both went there together on Thursday.
