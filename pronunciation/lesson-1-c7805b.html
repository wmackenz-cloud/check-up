My recordings · Pronunciation lesson 1

Reading Check

Goal: Record yourself reading a short text. We will use it to choose what to practise first. You will record it again in one month to hear your progress.

Time: about 10 minutes

What this lesson is for

Why this matters: people need to hear every word you say. Your first recording shows you and Bill which sounds make your English harder to follow, so you can practise the most useful things first.

You will know

  • the sounds that are often hard for Vietnamese and Chinese speakers: word endings, th and rhythm
  • which words in the text were difficult for you

You will understand

  • why a recording helps: you hear things you do not notice while you speak
  • why we start with the sounds that cause the most confusion

You will be able to

  • read a short text aloud without stopping
  • listen to yourself and find your difficult words
  • compare two recordings and hear your progress

Common mistakes to watch for

  • work worked, help helped: say the -ed at the end
  • fren friends, clo clothes: finish the word. Vietnamese speakers often drop the last sounds.
  • shop-uh shop: stop the word with no extra vowel. Chinese speakers often add one.
  • tree three, tink or sink think: put your tongue between your teeth
  • bu-si-ness (every syllable the same) BUSI-ness: make the strong syllable long

What to do

  1. Read the text below silently once.
  2. Read it aloud at your normal speed. Do not stop to correct yourself.
  3. Press Record, read the text, then press Stop.
  4. Listen, then press Save and send to Bill.

The text

Last summer I worked in a small shop. I talked to customers and helped them choose clothes. My friends think it's a boring job, but I liked it. Three days a week I started work at seven o'clock and finished at three. Next year I want to study business in the city, and I hope to open my own shop.

Listen to yourself

Play your recording once. Then answer these questions.

  1. Did you hear the end of every word? For example, worked, helped, friends, clothes.
  2. Did three and think sound different from tree and tink?
  3. Which word was the most difficult to say?

Practise your difficult words

Listen to your recording again. Tap every word that was difficult or did not sound right. Then press the button to practise them.
