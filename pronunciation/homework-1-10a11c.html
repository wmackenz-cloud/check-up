Goal: Hear and say the last sound of every word: -ed, -s, and the difference between bag and back.

Time: about 15 minutes. Do it once soon after our lesson, and do Parts 4 and 5 again the day before the next one. Goes with: Lesson 2: Finish the Word and Drill 1: Word Endings.

Part 2: How does the -ed sound?

Say each word out loud first. After t or d, -ed is an extra syllable: /ɪd/. After a quiet sound (p, k, s, sh, ch, f) it sounds like t. After a buzzing sound it sounds like d.
