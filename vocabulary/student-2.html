The rule

Some English words look or feel the same, but the meaning or the grammar is different.

Check two things: what does it mean? and what comes after it?

say something (to someone). tell someone something.

borrow = take for a short time. lend = give for a short time.

The pairs

Word Meaning and example
say / tell She said hello. She told me the news.
borrow / lend Can I borrow your pen? Can you lend me your pen?
bring / take Bring it here (towards me). Take it there (away from me).
remember / remind I remember her name. Remind me to call her.
lose / miss I lost my keys. I missed the bus. I miss my family.
rob / steal They robbed a bank. They stole money from the bank.
fun / funny The party was fun (enjoyable). The film was funny (made me laugh).
economic / economical economic growth (about the economy). An economical car (saves money).
sensible / sensitive A sensible decision (good judgement). Sensitive skin (reacts easily).
actual / current The actual price (real). The current price (now).
affect / effect Stress affects sleep (verb). Stress has an effect on sleep (noun).
rise / raise Prices rise (no object). They raised prices (with an object).

Watch out

  • She said me the answer. She told me the answer.
  • Can you borrow me some money? Can you lend me some money?
  • Please remember me to send the email. Please remind me to send the email.
  • Someone robbed my phone. Someone stole my phone.
  • The holiday was very funny. The holiday was a lot of fun.
