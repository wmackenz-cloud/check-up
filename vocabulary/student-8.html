The rule

A synonym is a word with the same or a very similar meaning.

Exams test this directly (Aptis: "choose the word that means the same") and in reading, where the text uses a different word from the question.

Synonyms are rarely exactly the same. Some are stronger (good → excellent) or more formal (buy → purchase).

Groups of synonyms, from weaker to stronger
