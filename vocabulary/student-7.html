The rule

Linking words show how two ideas connect. Check the meaning and the grammar after the word.

Contrast: but, however, although, even though, despite, in spite of, whereas, on the other hand

Reason and result: because, because of, so, therefore, as a result

Adding: and, also, in addition, moreover, as well as

Order: firstly, then, finally, to sum up

The grammar after them

Linking word What comes after Example
although / even though subject + verb Although it rained, we went out.
despite / in spite of noun or -ing Despite the rain, we went out. Despite feeling tired, I went out.
because subject + verb I stayed home because I was ill.
because of noun I stayed home because of my illness.
however new sentence, then a comma It was expensive. However, it was worth it.
therefore new sentence, then a comma He was late. Therefore, he missed the start.
whereas subject + verb (compares two things) My sister is quiet, whereas I am very talkative.

Watch out

  • Despite it was raining, we went out. Despite the rain / Although it was raining, we went out.
  • Although it was late but we continued. Although it was late, we continued. (Not although and but together.)
  • I was tired, however I finished. I was tired. However, I finished.
  • Because of I was ill, I stayed home. Because I was ill, I stayed home.

Exercise 1: Choose the correct linking word

  1. I like my job. (), the salary is low. (However / Although)
  2. () the traffic, we arrived on time. (Although / Despite)
  3. She passed the exam () she studied hard. (because / because of)
  4. The train was cancelled. (), we took a taxi. (Therefore / Whereas)
  5. () he is rich, he isn't happy. (Even though / In spite of)
  6. My brother loves football, () I prefer tennis. (whereas / therefore)

Exercise 2: Rewrite with despite

  1. Although it was cold, we went swimming.
  2. Although she was tired, she finished the report.
  3. Although he had a lot of experience, he didn't get the job.

Exercise 3: Join the sentences

Use the word in brackets.

  1. The hotel was expensive. The rooms were small. (and)
  2. I didn't go to work. I was ill. (because)
  3. The course was difficult. I enjoyed it. (although)
  4. Prices went up. Fewer people bought tickets. (as a result)
