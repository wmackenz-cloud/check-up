The rule

These verbs go with fixed partners. Learn the pair, not just the verb.

make = create or produce something: make a cake, make a mistake, make a decision, make money.

do = activities, work and jobs, often without a clear result: do homework, do the shopping, do your best, do business.

take = time, transport, tests, and many single actions: take a bus, take an exam, take a photo, take a break.

Watch out

  • I did a mistake. I made a mistake.
  • We have to make an exam. We have to take an exam.
  • Can you make me a favour? Can you do me a favour?
  • I want to do a photo. I want to take a photo.
  • The meeting will make place on Monday. The meeting will take place on Monday.
