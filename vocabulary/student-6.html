The rule

Choose your words for the reader.

Informal: to a friend or family member. Short everyday words, phrasal verbs, contractions (I'm, can't).

Formal: to a manager, a company, a teacher you don't know. Longer words, full forms (I am, cannot), no slang.

In Aptis and Cambridge writing, you often write one informal and one formal message. Using the right style gets you marks.
