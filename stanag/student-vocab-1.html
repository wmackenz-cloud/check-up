Exercise 4: Reading

A Day in the Life of a Company Commander

Captain Elena Moretti commands Bravo Company, part of an infantry battalion based in northern Italy. The company has about 120 soldiers organized into three platoons and a small headquarters section. Each platoon is led by a lieutenant, supported by an experienced sergeant.

Elena's day usually starts at 07:00 with a meeting with her platoon commanders and her company sergeant major. They review the training plan, discuss any problems with equipment or personnel, and agree on priorities. At 09:00 she reports to the battalion's commanding officer, a lieutenant colonel, to update him on the company's readiness.

“The hardest part of the job is not the training,” she says. “It's making sure information moves in both directions. My soldiers need to understand why we're doing something, and my CO needs to know about problems before they become serious.”

  1. How many platoons are in Bravo Company?
  2. Who leads each platoon?
  3. Who does Elena report to?
  4. What does she say is the hardest part of her job?
  5. Do you agree that communication is harder than training? Why?
