STANAG / JFLT Prep

Assessments and lessons for military and police students: the STANAG Placement Check for students preparing for the Italian JFLT (NATO STANAG 6001, Levels 1 to 3), and the English Placement Check B1 to C1 (CEFR) for professionals who are already past the basics, with a self-marking online version. Student versions are view-only links you can send; teacher versions stay private in your Google Drive. Assign them from the Assignments panel in your pipeline.
