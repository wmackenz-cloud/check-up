What this lesson is for

You will be able to

  • talk about your weekend, your last holiday or your last job
  • ask questions like Where did you go? and What did you buy?
  • make negative sentences with didn't + base verb

You will know

  • worked, lived, stopped, studied
  • went, bought, took, saw
  • yesterday, last week, two days ago

You will understand

  • I didn't go (not didn't went)
  • yesterday + past simple

Common mistakes to watch for

  • I didn't went to work yesterday. I didn't go to work yesterday.
  • Did you saw the game last night? Did you see the game last night?
  • I have visited Montreal last summer. I visited Montreal last summer.
  • She buyed a new coat on Saturday. She bought a new coat on Saturday.
  • Where you went on holiday? Where did you go on holiday?

How we use it

  • a finished action at a finished time: I called my sister yesterday.
  • a list of actions in a story, one after another: I got up, had a shower and left.
  • a past habit or state that is now finished: We lived in Ottawa for six years.

How to make it

PositiveI worked late. She went home. (regular verbs add -ed; irregular verbs change)
NegativeI didn't work late. She didn't go home. (didn't + base verb)
QuestionDid you work late? Where did she go?
Short answersYes, I did. No, she didn't.
Spellinglive → lived, stop → stopped, study → studied, play → played

Words that often go with it: yesterday, last night / week / year, two days ago, in 2015, on Monday, when I was a child, then, after that

Tip: Use the past form only once. In questions and negatives, did carries the past, so the main verb goes back to the base form: She went but Did she go? and She didn't go.
