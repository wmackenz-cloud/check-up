Quick reminder

  • Arrangements: plans with a time, a place or other people, often booked: I'm having lunch with Sara on Thursday.
  • Always with a future time, so the listener knows it is not now: We're flying to Montreal next Friday.
  • Timetables use the present simple: The bus leaves at 8:05. The film starts at 7.
  • Compare: I'm seeing the dentist at 3. (arranged) / I'm going to get fit. (intention) / I'll call you back. (decided now)
PositiveI'm meeting Tom at six. She's flying home on Sunday. They're coming tonight.
NegativeWe aren't coming to the party on Saturday. I'm not working tomorrow.
QuestionWhat are you doing tonight? Are you working on Sunday?
Short answersYes, I am. / No, we aren't.

Watch out for

  • I see the doctor tomorrow at 10. I'm seeing the doctor tomorrow at 10.
  • What do you do on Saturday evening? What are you doing on Saturday evening?
  • We flying to Calgary next Tuesday. We're flying to Calgary next Tuesday.
