Quick reminder

  • Instant decisions, made while you speak: The phone's ringing. I'll get it.
  • Offers and promises: I'll carry that for you. I won't tell anyone.
  • Predictions from opinion, often with I think or probably: I think it'll be a good party.
  • Requests: Will you close the door, please?
PositiveI / you / he / she / we / they will work (short form: I'll, she'll, we'll)
NegativeI won't work = I will not work
QuestionWill you help me? What will you do?
Short answersYes, I will. / No, I won't.

Watch out for

  • I will to phone you tonight. I will phone you tonight.
  • Will you to join us for lunch? Will you join us for lunch?
  • He wills be here soon. He will be here soon.
