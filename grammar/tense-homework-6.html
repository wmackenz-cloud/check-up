Quick reminder

  • an action in progress at a past time: At 7 p.m. I was driving home.
  • a longer action interrupted by a short one (past simple): I was cooking when the power went out.
  • the background to a story: It was raining and people were hurrying to work.
  • two actions happening at the same time: While I was working, the kids were playing.
PositiveI / he / she / it was working. You / we / they were working.
NegativeI wasn't sleeping. They weren't listening.
QuestionWere you sleeping? What was she doing?
Short answersYes, I was. No, they weren't.

Watch out for

  • I was knowing the answer. I knew the answer.
  • We was watching TV when you called. We were watching TV when you called.
  • When the phone rang, I cooked dinner. When the phone rang, I was cooking dinner.
