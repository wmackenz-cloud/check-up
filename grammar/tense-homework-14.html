Quick reminder

  • How long an action will have continued up to a future time: By June, I'll have been working here for ten years.
  • How long, up to a future event: When she retires, she'll have been teaching for 35 years.
  • To explain a future result: He'll be tired when he arrives. He'll have been driving all day.
PositiveI will have been working. / They'll have been waiting.
NegativeShe won't have been living here long.
QuestionHow long will you have been studying by June?
Short answersYes, I will. / No, I won't.

Watch out for

  • By June I will have been work here for ten years. By June I will have been working here for ten years.
  • By next month I will be living here for a year. By next month I will have been living here for a year.
  • By May I will have been knowing her for ten years. By May I will have known her for ten years.
