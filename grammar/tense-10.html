What this lesson is for

You will be able to

  • talk about your plans for the weekend, the year or your life
  • make predictions from what you can see now
  • ask What are you going to do? correctly

You will know

  • am / is / are going to + verb
  • Look!, Be careful!, I've decided

You will understand

  • going to for plans, will for decisions now
  • going to for what you can see coming

Common mistakes to watch for

  • I going to visit my parents on the weekend. I am going to visit my parents on the weekend.
  • She's going to looks for a new apartment. She's going to look for a new apartment.
  • Look at those clouds! It will rain. Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.
  • Do you going to take the course? Are you going to take the course?
  • I've bought running shoes because I will start jogging. I've bought running shoes because I'm going to start jogging.

How we use it

  • Plans and intentions you decided before speaking: I'm going to look for a new job this year.
  • Predictions from evidence you can see now: Look at that sky! It's going to rain.
  • Going to or will? A: We need eggs. B: I know. I'm going to buy some later. (already planned) / B: Oh! I'll buy some. (decided now)

How to make it

PositiveI am going to / he, she, it is going to / you, we, they are going to + base verb
NegativeI'm not going to stay. She isn't going to come. They aren't going to wait.
QuestionAre you going to call him? What is she going to do?
Short answersYes, I am. / No, she isn't.

Words that often go with it: tonight, this weekend, next month, one day, I've decided, I've already...; evidence words: Look!, Watch out!, Be careful!, Hurry up!

Tip: Ask yourself: did I decide before speaking? Use going to. Am I deciding now? Use will. Can I see it coming? Use going to.
