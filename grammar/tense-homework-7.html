Quick reminder

  • the earlier of two past actions: When we got to the cinema, the film had already started.
  • with by the time: By the time the ambulance came, he had stopped breathing.
  • to explain a reason in the past: She was upset because she had lost her ring.
  • experience up to a past moment: I had never eaten sushi before that night.
PositiveI / you / he / she / we / they had finished. Short form: I'd finished.
NegativeShe hadn't eaten. We had not seen it.
QuestionHad you met him before? Where had they gone?
Short answersYes, I had. No, they hadn't.

Watch out for

  • When I arrived, she already left. When I arrived, she had already left.
  • By the time we got to the station, the train left. By the time we got to the station, the train had left.
  • I had went there before. I had gone there before.
