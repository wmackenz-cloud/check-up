What this lesson is for

You will be able to

  • say what you and other people are doing at the moment
  • describe temporary situations: I'm working nights this month.
  • talk about changes: More people are working from home.

You will know

  • am / is / are + -ing
  • making, sitting, lying
  • state verbs: know, want, need, believe

You will understand

  • I work (habit) vs I'm working (now)
  • I know, not I'm knowing

Common mistakes to watch for

  • I working now. I am working now.
  • She is wear a red coat. She is wearing a red coat.
  • I am knowing her very well. I know her very well.
  • Look! It snows. Look! It's snowing.
  • What you are doing? What are you doing?

How we use it

  • actions happening now: Shh! The kids are sleeping.
  • temporary situations around now: I'm working nights this month.
  • changing trends: The city is getting busier.

How to make it

PositiveI'm working. She's working. They're working.
NegativeI'm not working. He isn't working. We aren't working.
QuestionAre you working? What is she doing?
Short answersYes, I am. No, I'm not. Yes, she is. No, she isn't.
Spellingtake → taking, swim → swimming, die → dying, study → studying

Words that often go with it: now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, this month, these days, currently, still, Look!, Listen!

Tip: Ask: is it happening now, or is it a habit? I drive to work (every day), but I'm taking the train this week (my car is at the garage). State verbs like know, want, believe stay simple, even now.
