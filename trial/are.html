Say More: Giving Your Opinion

What this lesson is for

Why this matters: a short answer is correct, but it often stops the conversation. With A.R.E. you give your opinion, a reason and an example, so you sound confident and the other person has more to talk about.

You will know

  • the three steps: Assertion, Reason, Evidence
  • useful starters: I think..., because..., For example...

You will understand

  • why a reason and an example make your answer stronger
  • that a short answer is a good start, not a mistake

You will be able to

  • turn a short answer into a longer, clearer one
  • give your opinion on everyday topics and keep a conversation going

Common mistakes to watch for

  • I am agree. I agree. (Agree is a verb, so no am.)
  • For me is better to live in a city. For me, it is better to live in a city. (English needs a subject.)
  • According to me, it's a good idea. In my opinion, it's a good idea. (Use according to for other people.)
  • In my opinion, I think it's good. I think it's good. (Use one starter, not two.)
  • For example my friend, he moved to Toronto. For example, my friend moved to Toronto. (Don't repeat the person with he.)

A short answer is a good start

"I like the city." That's correct English, but the conversation stops there. Here's a simple trick to say more, sound confident and keep the conversation going.

The A.R.E. trick

StepWhat you sayStart with
AAssertionYour opinionI think… / For me… / In my opinion…
RReasonWhy…because… / The main reason is…
EEvidenceAn exampleFor example… / In my experience… / Last year…

Example: Is it better to live in a big city or a small town?

A: I think a big city is better… R: …because there are more jobs. E: For example, my friend moved to Toronto and found a job in two weeks.

Let's talk

Pick a question. Answer with A.R.E., and then we'll chat about it.

  • What's the best way to spend a weekend?
  • Is it better to work from home or in an office?
  • Should everyone learn a second language?
  • Is it better to travel alone or with friends?
  • What's one thing that makes a good boss?

Remember

When someone asks what you think: Assertion, Reason, Evidence. Say what you think, say why, and give an example.
