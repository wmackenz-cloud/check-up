Military English

Eight vocabulary lessons for military, police and Guardia Civil students: ranks and chain of command, life on base, training, operations, radio, orders and briefings, maps and logistics, and police work. Each has an interactive student page with games and an answer key, teacher notes and 20 practice questions.
