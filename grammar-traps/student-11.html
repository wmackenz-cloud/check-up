Negative Infinitives: not to + verb

The rule

To say what someone should not do, put not in front of to: not + to + verb.

She told me not to call her. I decided not to buy it. I left early so as not to miss the bus.

Three things to remember:

  1. Not goes before to: not to go.
  2. Never add don't or doesn't. An infinitive has no helper verb.
  3. You will hear to not go in everyday speech. People understand it, but not to go is the safer choice for writing and exams.

Positive and negative

PositiveNegative
She told me to call her.She told me not to call her.
I decided to buy it.I decided not to buy it.
It's important to drive slowly here.It's important not to text while driving.
I left early so as to catch the bus.I left early so as not to miss the bus.

Verbs that often take a negative infinitive: advise, ask, tell, warn, remind, promise, decide, choose, try, agree, prefer, be careful, it's important / crucial / better.

Watch out

  • She decided to don't buy it. She decided not to buy it.
  • It is crucial no to touch the stove. It is crucial not to touch the stove.
  • He asked us to no speak. He asked us not to speak.
  • I try not eat sugar. I try not to eat sugar.
  • I want not to go. I don't want to go. (With want, need and like, make the main verb negative.)

Exercise 7: Speaking drills: answer without freezing

Hesitation usually comes from building each sentence from zero. These drills make not to automatic, so you can think about your ideas instead.

Drill 1: Repeat and speed up. Say each chunk three times: slow, normal, fast. not to worry · not to forget · not to be late · not to tell anyone · so as not to wake anyone

Drill 2: Swap one word. Your teacher says a new word. Change the sentence right away. She told me not to call her. → email → text → visit → wait for

Drill 3: Flip it. Your teacher says a positive sentence. Make it negative in under 3 seconds. "My boss asked me to stay late." → "My boss asked me not to stay late."

Drill 4: Quick questions. Start your answer within 3 seconds.

  • What did your parents always tell you not to do?
  • What is important not to do at a job interview?
  • What do you try not to do at the weekend?
  • What would you warn a tourist not to do in your country?

Drill 5: 3–2–1 talk. Talk about "Advice I'm glad I followed" for 3 minutes, then tell the same story in 2 minutes, then in 1 minute. Use not to at least three times.

When you need time to think, use a phrase, not silence:

  • "Let me think… my mother always told me not to…"
  • "That's a good question. I'd say it's important not to…"
  • "Hmm, the main thing is not to…"
