Present Simple or Present Continuous?

The rule

Ask yourself: is it always or usually true, or is it happening now or just for a short time?

Present simple (I work, she works): habits, routines, facts, things that are always true.

I drink coffee every morning. Water boils at 100 degrees.

Present continuous (am/is/are + -ing): actions happening now, or temporary situations around now.

I'm drinking tea at the moment. She's staying with friends this month.

State verbs describe thoughts, feelings and possession, not actions. We don't usually use them in the continuous.

I am knowing. I know. She is wanting a coffee. She wants a coffee.

Key words

Present simple Present continuous
always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day, on Mondays, once a week now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, these days, currently, Look! Listen!

State verbs to learn

Thinking Feeling Having Senses
know, believe, understand, remember, forget, mean, think (= opinion) like, love, hate, want, need, prefer have (= own), own, belong, contain seem, sound, smell, taste, look like

Careful: some verbs can be states or actions.

I think it's a good idea. (opinion) / I'm thinking about my holiday. (an activity)

I have a dog. (own) / I'm having lunch. (eating)

Watch out

  • I am going to the gym every day. I go to the gym every day.
  • Listen! The baby cries. Listen! The baby is crying.
  • I am understanding now. I understand now.
  • He is having three children. He has three children.
