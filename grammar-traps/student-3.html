The rule

Ask yourself three questions, in this order:

1. Does my listener know exactly which one? It is a specific one. Use the.

Can you close the door? (the door of this room) I bought a shirt. The shirt is blue. (I've already told you about it)

2. If not, is it one countable thing? It is any one, not a specific one. Use a or an.

I need a pen. She's an engineer.

3. Is it a plural or an uncountable noun, in general? Use nothing.

Dogs are friendly. Coffee is expensive. Life is short.

a or an? Listen to the first sound, not the letter: a university (you-), an hour (our-), an umbrella, a European country.

General or specific? A closer look

a = one of many, any one, or the first time you mention it. the = a specific one: we both know which one.

Kind of nounGeneral (any one, or all of them)Specific (we know which)
one countable thingWe want to buy a house. (any house)The house on the corner is for sale. (that one)
pluralHouses are expensive in Toronto. (all houses)The houses on my street are old. (those ones)
uncountable, something you can touchI drink coffee every morning. (coffee in general)The coffee in this café is excellent. (this coffee)
uncountable, an idea or feelingLove is important. (love in general)The love she shows her children is beautiful. (her love)

the + uncountable nouns. Uncountable nouns never take a or an, but they do take the when we point to a specific part or example of them.

  • Something real you can see or touch: Pass me the salt. The water in this lake is clean. Thanks for the information you sent.
  • An idea, feeling or time: She told me the truth. Thank you for the advice you gave me. Nobody knows what the future will bring. I'll never forget the peace of that morning.

Tip: words after the noun often make it specific: the coffee in this café, the history of Canada, the advice that you gave me. Compare History is interesting (in general) and The history of Canada is interesting (a specific history).

Jobs need a or an

She is doctor. She is a doctor. I am engineer. I am an engineer.

Watch out

  • The life is difficult. Life is difficult. (life in general)
  • I go to the work by the bus. I go to work by bus.
  • He is best player in team. He is the best player in the team.
  • I love the music. I love music. (all music, in general)
