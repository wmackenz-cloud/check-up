Similes

Six units for very advanced (C1/C2) students: fixed similes with as ... as and like, ironic and humorous similes, and how to make fresh similes of your own. Each has expression cards, examples from literature, poetry and music, seven interactive exercises with an answer key, and a separate homework drill.

This page is for you. Send students the unit link for the lesson, and the homework link after it.
