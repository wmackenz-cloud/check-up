Quick reminder

In English, progress is movement along a path. A goal is a destination, a plan is a route, problems are obstacles, choices are forks in the road, and how far we have got is distance travelled.

  • at a crossroads: at a point where an important decision must be made
  • a dead end: a situation or path with no possibility of progress
  • an uphill battle: a struggle that is very hard to win
  • on the right track: doing things in a way that will probably succeed
  • light at the end of the tunnel: a sign that a long difficult period is ending
  • cross that bridge when we come to it: deal with a problem only when it actually happens
  • go the extra mile: make more effort than is expected
  • come a long way: make a lot of progress

Watch out for

  • We'll cross that bridge when we arrive to it. We'll cross that bridge when we come to it. The idiom is fixed.
  • She went the extra kilometre for her clients. She went the extra mile. Even in metric Canada, the idiom keeps mile.
  • I'm in a crossroads in my career. I'm at a crossroads in my career. We stand at a point on a road.
