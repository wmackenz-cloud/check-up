Before you start

Music makes us feel things. In this lesson you listen to seven short clips and learn the words to describe them.

Use headphones if you can. Each clip is less than a minute long. Listen as many times as you like.

The rule: -ing or -ed?

-ing describes the music (the thing). -ed describes how you feel (the person).

The music is relaxing. I feel relaxed.

The ending is exciting. I get excited.

The song is boring. I am bored.

  • I am boring. I am bored. (Unless you want to say people don't enjoy talking to you!)
  • The film was very excited. The film was very exciting.
  • I'm interesting in jazz. I'm interested in jazz.

The rule: strong adjectives

Some adjectives are already very strong. Use absolutely with them, not very.

very good → absolutely fantastic · very sad → absolutely heartbreaking · very loud → absolutely deafening

Use very with ordinary adjectives: very calm, very lively. Really works with both: really calm, really fantastic.

very fantastic · absolutely calm

Exercise 8: Speaking

Play a clip for your teacher or a friend. Then talk about it:

  • What does it sound like? It sounds absolutely terrifying.
  • How does it make you feel? It makes me feel a bit nervous.
  • Where could you hear this music? In a film? At a wedding? In a shop?
  • Which clip did you like most? Which did you like least? Why?
