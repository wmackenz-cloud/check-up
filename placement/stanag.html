STANAG Placement Check

This check helps your tutor estimate your English level in reading, listening and writing, on the NATO STANAG 6001 scale, Levels 1 to 3. It is a practice estimate, not the official JFLT. Each part gets harder as you go. Do your best, and if a section feels much too difficult, move on to the next part.

  • Total time: about 70 minutes (Reading 25, Listening 15, Writing 30). You can do it in two sessions.
  • Your answers are saved in this browser as you go, so you can stop and come back later on the same device.
  • Please don't use a dictionary, a translator or AI tools. We want to see what you know today.
  • The reading and listening parts are marked on the page. Your tutor marks the writing.
  • The result is an estimated level to help plan your lessons. It is not an official exam result.

Part 1

Reading

Read each text and choose the best answer (a, b, c or d).

When you have answered all the questions, press "Mark my reading" at the bottom of the page.

Part 2

Listening

You can play each recording twice. Read the questions before you listen.

You can't skip forward or back in a recording, so listen carefully. You can answer while you listen and after the second play. When you have finished all three sections, press "Mark my listening".

Part 3

Writing

Complete as many tasks as you can. Plan for a few minutes before you write.

Your tutor, Bill, will read and mark your writing. Nothing here is marked automatically. Your writing is saved in this browser as you type, so you can stop and come back.

Send your work to Bill

When you have finished, press "Copy my writing". It copies your three texts and your reading and listening results. Then paste them into a Preply message to Bill, or an email to wmackenz@gmail.com.
