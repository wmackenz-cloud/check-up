Tourism English

2 lessons for meeting tourists and for being one: welcoming visitors and helping them, then asking for directions, tickets and information on holiday. Both practise asking questions: closed (yes/no and either/or) and open (where, what, how). Each has an interactive student page (games, a reading and an answer key), teacher notes with a 50-minute plan, and 20 practice questions.

This page is for you. Send students the student worksheet link for the lesson you're teaching.

  1. 1
    Welcoming Tourists
    Greeting visitors and offering help; closed questions (Is this your first visit? Would you like a map or a guide?) and open questions (Where are you from? What would you like to see?)
    Student worksheet Teacher notes
  2. 2
    Being a Tourist
    Directions, tickets, prices and opening times; closed questions (Can I pay by card?), open questions (How do I get to ...?) and polite Could you tell me ...?
