What this lesson is for

Why this matters: meetings are where decisions are made at work. With the right words and polite phrases, you can follow the discussion, give your opinion and be heard.

You will know

  • meeting words: agenda, item, minutes, action points, deadline, AOB
  • verbs that go with meeting: hold, attend, postpone, cancel, chair
  • phrases to give an opinion, agree, disagree and interrupt

You will understand

  • why I see your point, but ... works better than a direct No
  • why minutes is always plural and attend has no to

You will be able to

  • give your opinion, agree and disagree politely
  • interrupt politely and check that you understand
  • read the minutes and send a short email with the action points

Common mistakes to watch for

  • I am agree with Sofia. I agree with Sofia. (agree is a verb: no am)
  • Let's discuss about the price. Let's discuss the price. (no preposition after discuss)
  • Ten people assisted to the meeting. Ten people attended the meeting. (assist means help)
  • Can you send me the minute? Can you send me the minutes? (always plural)
  • We didn't arrive to a decision. We didn't reach a decision.
  • Sorry for interrupt, but ... Sorry to interrupt, but ...

Warm-up

  • How many meetings do you have in a normal week? Are they online or in a room?
  • What makes a meeting good? What makes it a waste of time?
  • In a meeting in English, what is hardest for you: following the discussion, giving your opinion, or interrupting?

Key vocabulary

Meeting words

WordMeaning
agendathe list of topics for the meeting
itemone topic on the agenda (item 3 on the agenda)
minutesthe written notes of what was said and decided (always plural)
action pointsthe tasks that people must do after the meeting
chairthe person who runs the meeting; also a verb: to chair a meeting
attendeesthe people at the meeting
deadlinethe date or time when a task must be finished
AOB (any other business)the last item: other topics that are not on the agenda

Verbs with meeting

VerbMeaningExample
hold / have a meetinga meeting takes placeWe hold a team meeting every Monday.
attend a meetinggo to it (no to after attend)Twelve people attended the meeting.
set up / schedule a meetingarrange it and choose a timeCan we set up a meeting for Thursday?
postpone / put off a meetingmove it to a later timeWe've postponed the meeting until next week.
cancel a meetingdecide that it will not happenThe meeting is cancelled.
chair / run a meetinglead it and keep it on timeLaura is chairing today's meeting.
reach a decision / an agreementfinally decide or agreeWe didn't reach a decision.
raise an issue / a pointstart talking about a problem or topicI'd like to raise an issue about parking.

Useful phrases

JobPhrase
StartShall we get started? / Let's begin with item one.
Give an opinionIn my opinion, ... / I think we should ...
AgreeI agree with you. / That's a good point. / Exactly.
Disagree politelyI see your point, but ... / I'm not sure I agree.
InterruptSorry to interrupt, but ... / Could I just add something?
Check you understandCould you clarify that? / So what you're saying is ...?
Move onLet's move on to the next item.
FinishTo sum up, ... / Let's finish there. Thanks, everyone.
