Business English

Five lessons for adult professionals in any workplace: talking about your company and job, meetings, emails and phone calls, numbers and trends, and negotiating. Each has an interactive student page (games, a reading, Claude writing checks and an answer key), teacher notes with a 50-minute plan, and 20 practice questions.
