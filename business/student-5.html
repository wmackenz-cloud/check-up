What this lesson is for

Why this matters: negotiating is a normal part of business, from prices to delivery dates. Polite, clear phrases help you ask for more, say no without causing offence, and close a fair deal.

You will know

  • negotiation words: offer, proposal, terms, discount, compromise, condition, deal, contract
  • useful phrases: If you ..., we can ...; Would you be willing to ...?; I'm afraid we can't ...
  • word partners: make an offer, reach an agreement, close a deal, sign a contract

You will understand

  • why we say If you order, not If you will order
  • why polite phrases help both sides reach an agreement

You will be able to

  • make an offer and a conditional offer
  • ask for better terms and say no politely
  • suggest a compromise and confirm the agreement

Common mistakes to watch for

  • If you will pay now, we give you 5% off. If you pay now, we can give you 5% off. (no will after if)
  • We can make you a discount. We can give you a discount. / We can offer you a discount.
  • Who will firm the contract? Who will sign the contract?
  • Would you be willing to accepting this? Would you be willing to accept this?
  • We must do a decision today. We must make a decision today.
  • The price is too expensive. The price is too high. (a product is expensive; a price is high)

Warm-up

  • Do you negotiate in your job? With whom: clients, suppliers, your manager?
  • When did you last negotiate a price in your own life (a car, an apartment, at a market)? What happened?
  • In English, what is harder for you: asking for more, or saying no?

Key vocabulary

Negotiation words

WordMeaning
negotiatediscuss something to reach an agreement
offerwhat you are ready to give (noun and verb: make an offer, we can offer you ...)
proposala plan or offer that you present, often in writing
termsthe conditions of a deal: price, payment, delivery
discounta lower price than usual: a 10% discount
compromisean agreement where both sides give something
conditionsomething that must happen first: on one condition: ...
dealan agreement, especially in business: It's a deal!
contracta written legal agreement

Useful phrases

JobPhrase
Make an offerWe can offer you a 5% discount.
Make a conditional offerIf you order 500 units, we can give you 8% off.
Ask for moreCould you do a little better on the price?
Ask politelyWould you be willing to pay in advance?
Say no politelyI'm afraid we can't go that low.
Suggest a compromiseHow about if we meet in the middle?
Check the detailsSo, just to confirm: ...
AgreeThat sounds fair. / It's a deal.

Verb + noun

Verb + nounExample
make a decision / an offerWe need to make a decision by Friday.
reach an agreementAfter two hours, we reached an agreement.
accept / reject an offerThey rejected our first offer.
close a dealShe closed the deal on Monday.
sign a contractWe'll sign the contract next week.
meet a deadlineCan you meet the deadline of May 1?

Exercise 5: Reading

Hannah (buyer, Maple Hotels): Thanks for your proposal, Carlos. We like the quality of the towels, but $12 each is too high for us.

Carlos (sales manager, SoftLine): I understand. What price did you have in mind?

Hannah: Our budget is around $10.

Carlos: I'm afraid we can't go that low. But if you order 2,000 towels instead of 1,500, we can offer $11.

Hannah: That's interesting. Would you be willing to include free delivery?

Carlos: We could do that on one condition: you pay within 15 days, not 30.

Hannah: Hmm. How about if we pay within 20 days?

Carlos: OK, let's meet in the middle. 20 days it is.

Hannah: Great. So, just to confirm: 2,000 towels at $11 each, free delivery, and payment within 20 days.

Carlos: That's right. I'll send you the contract this afternoon.

Hannah: It's a deal.
