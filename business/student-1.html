Goal: talk about your company, your department and your job: what the company does, how it is organized, and what you are responsible for (work for / in / on, be responsible for, be in charge of, report to, deal with, my job involves + -ing).

Warm-up

  • What does your company (or your last company) do? Is it big or small?
  • What is your job title? How would you explain it to someone who doesn't know your industry?
  • In English, what is harder for you: saying what your company does, or explaining what you do every day?

Key vocabulary

Talking about your job

PhraseUse it forExample
work for + companyyour employerI work for a logistics company in Warsaw.
work in + department / fieldthe part of the company, or the industryI work in the finance department. She works in IT.
work on + projecta project or task you are doing nowThis month I'm working on a new website.
be responsible for + noun / -ingyour dutiesI'm responsible for hiring new staff.
be in charge ofa team, an office or an area you manageShe's in charge of the Toronto office.
report to + personyour manager (the person above you)I report to the sales director.
deal with / handleproblems, people or tasks that come to youI deal with customer complaints.
run / managelead a business, a team or a projectHe runs a small team of six.
my job involves + -ingwhat your work includesMy job involves travelling a lot.

The company

WordMeaning
head office / headquartersthe main office of a company, where the top managers work
branchone office or shop of a bigger company, in another place
subsidiarya company that is owned by a bigger company
departmenta part of a company that does one type of work
staff / employeesthe people who work for a company (staff has no -s: 300 staff)
colleaguea person you work with
client / customera client buys a service (from a lawyer, an agency); a customer buys products
suppliera company that sells you goods, materials or services
competitora company that sells the same things as you

Departments

DepartmentWhat it does
Human Resources (HR)hires staff, and deals with pay, training and holidays
Finance / Accountsmoney: invoices, payments, budgets and taxes
Salessells products or services to customers
Marketingadvertising, the brand and market research
Customer Serviceanswers customers' questions and complaints
ITcomputers, networks and software
Logisticsmoves and stores the goods
Purchasingbuys materials and services from suppliers

Say it right: COLleague, reSPONsible, subSIDiary, headQUARters, PURchasing (chase sounds like chuss).

Exercise 7: Prepositions and mistakes

Part A: for, of or to?

Watch out

  • for: work for, responsible for, apply for
  • of: in charge of, head of, part of
  • to: report to, talk to, send something to, introduce someone to
