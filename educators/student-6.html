What this lesson is for

Why this matters: meetings with parents and job interviews are important moments. Positive, polite language helps you build trust with families and show your experience clearly to an interview panel.

You will know

  • US and UK words: parent-teacher conference / parents' evening, teachers' lounge / staff room, résumé / CV
  • job words: vacancy, post, referees, cover letter
  • polite phrases to start with a strength, raise a concern and ask for support

You will understand

  • why we use the present perfect for experience up to now: I've been teaching for twelve years
  • why gentle phrases like She doesn't always ... work better than She never ...

You will be able to

  • talk to parents about progress and concerns in a positive way
  • describe your experience in a job interview
  • write a warm, professional email to a parent

Common mistakes to watch for

  • To an American colleague: What are you doing in the summer holidays? What are you doing for summer vacation? (In the US, a holiday is a special day, like Thanksgiving, and the holidays means Christmas and New Year. In the UK, holiday means any time off.)
  • I attach my curriculum. I attach my CV (UK) / résumé (US). (In Spanish, Italian and Portuguese, curriculum also means your CV. In English, it does not.)
  • I work at this school since 2018. I have worked (or have been working) at this school since 2018.
  • I'd like to apply to the vacancy. I'd like to apply for the vacancy.
  • I'm very interested on this job. I'm very interested in this job.
  • Could you pass out the worksheets? Could you hand out the worksheets? (pass out also means faint)

Warm-up

  • How often do you talk to parents? In person, online, or by message?
  • What is the hardest thing to say to a parent?
  • Have you ever had a job interview in English? How did it go?

Language point: polite, positive and professional

PurposeUseful phrases
Start with a strengthYour child is making good progress in ... One thing she does really well is ...
Raise a concern gentlyOne area we're working on is ... He sometimes finds it hard to ... She doesn't always ... (softer than She never ...)
Ask for supportI'd love your support at home with ... Could we arrange a time to talk? Would you be able to ...?
Your experience: present perfect for up to nowI've taught / I've been teaching for twelve years. I've worked with students aged 11 to 18.
A finished job or time: past simpleIn my last school, I was responsible for Year 6. In 2019 I moved to Kraków.
Selling yourselfA strength I'd bring to your team is ... I'm particularly interested in ... I'd welcome the chance to ...
