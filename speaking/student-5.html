What this lesson is for

Why this matters: in a meeting or a conversation, people remember how you finish. When you end with your point again, your opinion sounds complete and the listener remembers it.

You will know

  • the four PREP steps: Point, Reason, Example, Point again
  • phrases for each step: In my opinion... / The main reason is... / For example... / So that's why...

You will understand

  • why saying your point again at the end makes your answer stronger
  • how to notice when a step is missing

You will be able to

  • give your opinion clearly and finish it strongly
  • reply to an announcement in a meeting with PREP

Common mistakes to watch for

  • stopping after the example, then silence Finish with your point: So that's why I think...
  • starting with “Because...” Say your point first, then the reason.
  • repeating your first sentence word for word Say your point again in new words: So overall...
  • The main reason is because many people work all week. The main reason is that many people work all week.
  • adding “and also... and another thing...” after your final point When you have said your final point, stop. You have finished.

Time: 50-minute lesson, then 5 minutes of homework.

From A.R.E. to PREP

A.R.E. helps you start strong. PREP adds one more step: say your point again at the end. Then the listener knows you have finished, and they remember your idea.

Part A: The PREP method

PPointSay your opinion.I think... / In my opinion...
RReasonSay why....because... / The main reason is...
EExampleGive an example.For example... / In my experience...
PPointSay your opinion again, in new words.So that's why I think... / That's why... / So overall...

Question: Should people work four days a week?

  • P: I think a four-day week is a good idea...
  • R: ...because people are more focused when they are not tired.
  • E: For example, my sister's company tried it last year, and nobody missed a deadline.
  • P: So that's why I think more companies should try it.
