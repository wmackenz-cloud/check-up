Rescue Phrases

Take your time: nothing here is timed. Green dot = right first time. Get a gold star for each perfect exercise. Your progress stays on this device.

Goal: Learn phrases that keep a conversation going when you freeze.

Time: 50-minute lesson, then 5 minutes a day

When you freeze, you do not need to stop

Use a rescue phrase.

It fills the silence, tells the other person what is happening, and gives you time.

Part A: I don't understand

  • Sorry, could you say that again?
  • Could you speak a little more slowly, please?
  • What does "___" mean?
  • Do you mean...?

Part B: I can't find the word

  • I don't know the word in English, but it's like a...
  • It's the thing you use to...
  • How do you say...?
  • It's a kind of...

Tip: you do not need the exact word. Describe it, and the other person will often help you.

Part C: I need a moment

  • Let me think.
  • That's a good question.
  • Just a second.
  • Hmm, how can I say this?
  • Give me a second to find the right word.

Part D: I made a mistake

  • Sorry, I mean...
  • Let me say that again.
  • No, wait. What I mean is...
  • Actually, let me put it this way...
