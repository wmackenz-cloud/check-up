Today's goal

Tell a short story about your life in a clear order, from beginning to end.

Time: 50-minute lesson, then 5 minutes of homework.

A story needs a map

When people tell a story, they often get lost in the details and forget the ending. STAR is your map: four steps, always in the same order.

Part A: The STAR method

SSituationWhere and when? Who was there?Last year... / A few months ago... / When I was working at...
TTaskWhat did you need to do? What was the problem?I had to... / The problem was... / My job was to...
AActionWhat did you do?So I... / First, I... Then I... / I decided to...
RResultWhat happened in the end?In the end... / As a result... / I learned that...

Part B: Example

Question: Tell me about a time something went wrong when you were travelling.

  • S: Two years ago, I was travelling to Madrid for a work meeting.
  • T: My flight was cancelled, and I had to be at the meeting the next morning.
  • A: So I went to the airline desk and asked about other flights. They found me a seat on a later flight, and I booked a hotel near the office.
  • R: In the end, I arrived at midnight, but I was at the meeting on time. I learned to always travel a day early.

Part C: Put it in order

Give each sentence a number from 1 to 4. Tap the number. Then tell the story in your own words.

  1. So I called my neighbour, who has a spare key, and she let me in.
  2. Last winter, I came home late after work. It was very cold.
  3. In the end, I was inside in ten minutes. Now I always leave a spare key with a friend.
  4. I couldn't find my keys, and I needed to get into my flat.

Part C practice: Match the steps

Here is a new story: Tell me about a time you helped someone. Tap a STAR step, then tap its sentence.

  • S: Situation = Last summer, I was waiting for a bus in the city centre.
  • T: Task = An old man was lost, and he needed to find the hospital.
  • A: Action = So I looked at the map on my phone and walked there with him.
  • R: Result = In the end, he arrived on time, and I learned that a little help matters.

Part C practice 2: Task or Action?

The Task is what you needed to do, or the problem. The Action is what you actually did. Tap Task or Action.

  1. "I had to finish the report by Monday." →
  2. "So I asked my colleague for help." →
  3. "The problem was that the shop was closed." →
  4. "First, I called the hotel. Then I sent an email." →
  5. "My job was to welcome the new students." →
  6. "I decided to take a taxi." →
  7. "I needed to find a new flat quickly." →
  8. "So I looked online every evening." →

Part D practice: Write your STAR story

Choose one prompt from Part D. Write your story, one step in each box. One or two short sentences is enough. Press Check after each box, and Claude will tell you if it fits the step.

  1. S: Situation. Where and when? Who was there? (Last year... / A few months ago...)

  3. A: Action. What did you do? (So I... / First, I... Then I...)
  4. R: Result. What happened in the end? (In the end... / I learned that...)

Part E: STAR inside PREP

A short story is the best example. Put a STAR story inside your PREP answer:

  • P: I think...
  • R: ...because...
  • E: For example, last year... (a short STAR story)
  • P: So that's why I think...
