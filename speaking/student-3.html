Goal: Give yourself time to think while you are speaking, and keep your sentences simple and clear.

Time: 50-minute lesson, then 5 minutes of homework

Fluent speakers are not perfect

They use small tools to keep talking.

You can use the same tools. Fillers and easy starts are not mistakes: fluent speakers use them all the time.

Part A: Fillers

These words give you a few seconds to think.

  • Well...
  • So...
  • Um... / Uh...
  • You know...
  • Let me see...
  • Actually...

Part B: Easy starts

Begin your sentence before you know the end.

  • I think...
  • For me, ...
  • In my experience, ...
  • The thing is, ...
  • To be honest, ...
  • One example is...

Part C: Simple is fine

Long sentences are hard to build while you speak. Short sentences are clear and easy to follow. Read both columns aloud.

Instead of thisTry this
The reason that I decided to start learning English was because my company was expanding.My company was growing. They needed English speakers. So I started learning.
If I had more time, I would probably travel more often to visit my family.I want to travel more. My family is far away. But I don't have time.
The apartment that I am living in now is bigger than the one where I lived before.I moved last year. My new apartment is bigger. I like it more.

Exercise 3: Make it short

Each long sentence is hard to say. Build the short version: tap the words in order. Tap a word in your sentence to send it back.

  1. The thing that I like most about my job is that every day is different.
  2. Although the weather in my city is often cold, I still enjoy walking there.
  3. The reason why I was late this morning was because the bus did not come.
  4. If I had known that the shop was closed on Sundays, I would not have gone there.

Exercise 4: Your turn to make it short

Write each long sentence again as two, three or four short sentences. Keep the same meaning. Then say your short version aloud.

  1. The reason that I like living in my neighbourhood is that there is a park where I can walk my dog every morning.
  2. When I was a child, my family, who lived in a small town near the mountains, used to go camping every summer.
  3. The job that I had before this one was more interesting, but the salary that they paid me was lower.

The 4-3-2 talk

In the lesson, you will speak about one topic three times, and each time will be shorter. Bill keeps the time, so you only need to talk. Use your fillers and easy starts.

Notice how much easier the third time feels. What changed?
