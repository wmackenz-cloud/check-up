What this lesson is for

Why this matters: when someone asks your opinion, a very short answer can sound weak or unfinished. A.R.E. gives you a simple plan, so you know what to say next and your answer sounds clear and confident.

You will know

  • the three parts of A.R.E.: Assertion, Reason, Evidence
  • useful words for each part: I believe... / ...because... / For example...

You will understand

  • why a reason and an example make your opinion stronger
  • that any opinion is fine: what matters is that you explain it

You will be able to

  • give your opinion clearly in three steps
  • build an A.R.E. answer to questions about work and daily life

Common mistakes to watch for

  • answering only “Yes” or “I don't know” Say what you think, even in a few words: I think...
  • giving your opinion and then stopping Add a reason (because...) and an example (For example...).
  • worrying that your opinion is “wrong” Any opinion is fine. Just explain it.
  • I am agree. I agree.
  • In my opinion, I think working from home is better. In my opinion, working from home is better. (Use one, not both.)
  • For example, my friend he moved to Toronto. For example, my friend moved to Toronto. (Don't add he.)

Time: 25-minute lesson, then 1 minute of homework

A short answer is not enough

“I like the city because it has restaurants.”

This is correct, but it is not very strong. Use A.R.E. to make your answer complete and confident.

Part B: Example

Question: Is it better to live in a big city or a small village?

  • A: I believe living in a big city is better...
  • R: ...because there are far more career and networking opportunities.
  • E: For example, my friend moved to Toronto and found a marketing job within two weeks.

Exercise 2: Match the opinion and the reason

Tap an opinion (A), then tap the reason (R) that goes with it.

  • I think it's better to rent a home... ...because you can move easily for a new job.
  • In my opinion, people shouldn't retire at 60... ...because many people still love their work at that age.
  • I believe history is important to learn... ...because it helps us understand the world today.
  • I think tourists should pay extra in busy cities... ...because the cities need money to clean and repair the streets.
  • In my opinion, travelling with friends is better... ...because you can share the costs and the memories.

Exercise 3: Build an A.R.E. answer

Put the words in order to make a full answer: A, then R, then E. Tap a word in your answer to send it back.

  2. Are dogs good pets?
