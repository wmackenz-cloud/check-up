Today's goal

Practise a real situation you find difficult, using all your new tools.

Time: 50-minute lesson.

Part A: My toolkit

  • Rescue phrases: for when I don't understand, can't find a word, need a moment or make a mistake
  • Fillers: Well... So... Let me see...
  • Easy starts: I think... For me... The thing is...
  • Simple is fine: short, clear sentences
  • Giving an opinion: A.R.E. (Assertion, Reason, Evidence) or PREP (Point, Reason, Example, Point)
  • Telling a story: STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
