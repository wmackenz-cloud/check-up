Speaking Confidence

A 7-lesson series for students who freeze when they speak: naming the freeze, rescue phrases, thinking time, A.R.E., PREP, STAR and a real-world rehearsal. Each lesson has an interactive student page (with games, an answer key and Claude writing checks) and teacher notes with the lesson plan.
