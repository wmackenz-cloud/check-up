2. The vocabulary

Read each word, its meaning and the examples. Then make your own sentence with each word and say it out loud.

check in

officially tell the plane company that you will get on the plane, often giving them your luggage at the same time

boarding pass

ticket used to get on the plane, showing your seat number

go through security

pass through an area where you are checked for anything that you shouldn't carry on a plane

boarding gate

area where you wait just before you get on the plane

catch a flight

successfully get on a plane

miss a flight

don't get on a plane that you planned to travel on, often because you are too late
