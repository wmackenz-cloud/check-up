2. The vocabulary

Read each word, its meaning and the examples. Then make your own sentence with each word and say it out loud.

Nutrients

important substances that all lifeforms need to survive and be healthy

Synonyms: nourishment, sustenance, fuel

Opposite: junk food

How to use it:

  • its full of nutrients
  • a nutritious meal

Saturated

fats that are thought to be worse for our health, often found in meat and dairy

Synonyms: soaked, drenched, full to the brim

How to use it:

  • the ground is saturated with water
  • the job market is saturated with applicants

Deep sleep

stage of the sleep cycle when the brain is at its slowest and the body can rest and repair

Synonyms: profound, bottomless

How to use it:

  • I had a deep sleep last night.
  • I slept deeply.
  • I was deeply moved, the team dug deep

Supplements

tablets and other consumables that contain particular nutrients that you might not get in your everyday diet

Synonyms: extra, additional

How to use it:

  • I have a supplement of vitamin c every day.
  • I supplement my income with a job on the weekends.
  • It is a supplemental role.

Fed information

give lots of information continuously

Synonyms: coached, brainwashed, spoon fed

How to use it:

  • He was fed information about the election.
  • The recipient is passively given information typically from one source.
  • The source fed information to the reporter.

Magic bullet

simple and effective solution, usually one that doesn't exist in reality

Synonyms: quick fix, magic formula

How to use it:

  • Comes from the cure for syphilis in early 1900.
  • They thought they had found a magic bullet for curing cancer.
