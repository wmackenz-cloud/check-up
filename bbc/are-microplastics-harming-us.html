2. The vocabulary

Read each word, its meaning and the examples. Then make your own sentence with each word and say it out loud.

marine

related to, found in or produced by the sea

Synonyms: oceanic, undersea, underwater

emerging

coming into view after being hidden, allowing a better understanding or development

Synonyms: developing, embryonic

correlation

relationship between two or more things

Synonyms: related, connected, association

quantified

measured and given a number or a value

Synonyms: measure, calcultated

exposure

come into contact with something that may have an impact on you

Synonyms: risk, jeopardy vulnerable
