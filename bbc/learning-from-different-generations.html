2. The vocabulary

Read each word, its meaning and the examples. Then make your own sentence with each word and say it out loud.

generation

group of people who are of a similar age

Synonyms: cohort, contemporaries baby boomer ( 1945 -1960)

How to use it:

  • it’s generational, it’s a generational change

fill a void

help to feel better after experiencing loss

Synonyms: close the gap, plug up gap

such and such

reference to something without giving a specific example

Synonyms: this and that

hear on the grapevine I heard it on the grape vine

hear a rumour gossiping

Synonyms: rumour mill, word of mouth, scuttlebut

stereotypes

Commonly held ideas about a group of people that may or may not be true.

Synonyms: cliche, preconception

lively

loud and with lots of energy

Synonyms: vivacious, spirited
