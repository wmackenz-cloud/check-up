chronic

continuing for a long time

Synonyms: persistent, constant

How to use it:

  • she has a chronic cough, she is chronically in love

burnout

physical or mental exhaustion caused by working too hard

Synonyms: exhausted, fatigued

How to use it:

  • She works too hard she is suffering from burnout

nerves

Synonyms: jitters, anxiety, boldness, guts

How to use it:
