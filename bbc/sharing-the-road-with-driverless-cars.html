Eye contact

look directly into someone's eyes

Synonyms: gaze, eye to eye

How to use it:

  • He made eye contact, they locked eyes, they see eye to eye

My sentence:

Wave

raise your hand and move it from side to side as a greeting or to communicate with someone

Synonyms: gesture, signal

How to use it:

  • He waved down a taxi
  • He waved off the accusation

My sentence:

Alert

warn someone of potential danger, to be aware of your surroundings

Synonyms: watchful, attentive, wary, vigilant

How to use it:

  • He is very alert this morning
  • They sent out an alert to warn people about the weather

My sentence:

Animation

display of moving images, to be full of energy

Synonyms: vitality, verve vitality

How to use it:

  • He was very animated

My sentence:

Glance

quick and brief look

Synonyms: scan, peek, peep, glimpse

How to use it:

  • to strike at an angle (glanced off of)
  • I only glanced at it, I had a quick glance

My sentence:

Jaywalking

(American English) crossing the road where it is not allowed or without taking care

Synonyms: cut across, shortcut

My sentence:

Pedestrian

A person walking on the sidewalk or street, a boring everyday thing

Synonyms: walker, person, banal, boring, humdrum

How to use it:

  • His ideas are pedestrian

My sentence:

Autonomous

Done without human involvement, done by a machine

Synonyms: independent, self-governing, free

How to use it:

  • Singapore is an autonomous city it is independent sovereign city.
